COMMON QUESTIONS

Lead generation questions

Does lead generation guarantee a fixed number of leads?

No. Enquiry volume and quality depend on the audience, offer, channel, budget, landing page and follow-up. A plan should set a clear objective and review what the actual campaign data shows.

What information should an enquiry form ask for?

Ask only for details needed to respond and qualify the request, such as contact information and the service the person is interested in. Explain why you need the information and what happens after submission.

What should happen after someone submits an enquiry?

Show a clear confirmation, make sure the enquiry reaches the right person, and follow up within a reasonable time. Tracking the source and outcome of enquiries can help you decide what to improve.
