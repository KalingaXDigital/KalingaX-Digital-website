HOW WE WORK

Clear steps.
Thoughtful work.

Open each step to see how we learn about your business, plan the right direction and shape the digital work around your goals.

01Understand+

We start by learning about your business and what you want to achieve. We discuss:

  • What products or services you offer and what makes them useful to customers.
  • Who you want to reach, where your audience is based, and what they need help with.
  • What is already working online and where visitors or enquiries are getting stuck.
  • Which outcome matters most right now, such as a clearer website, stronger social presence or more enquiries.

This gives us a shared picture of the audience, priorities and project scope before work begins.

02Plan+

We turn the conversation into a practical direction that fits your needs and available information. Planning can include:

  • Selecting the relevant support: website or app development, branding, social media, Meta advertising or lead generation.
  • Organizing page structure, key messages, content and the actions visitors should take.
  • Agreeing the project priorities, information needed from you and a realistic sequence of work.
  • For campaigns, aligning the audience, offer, creative direction and enquiry destination.

We review the proposed direction with you so the work can move ahead with clear expectations.

03Create+

We develop the agreed digital work in clear stages and keep it connected to the original goal. Depending on the project, this may include:

  • Creating the website or app structure, visual design and responsive page layouts.
  • Preparing brand assets, social content or campaign creative in the agreed direction.
  • Setting up relevant calls to action, contact paths and enquiry forms.
  • Reviewing content, links and layouts with you, then making agreed refinements before launch or handover.

After delivery, we can discuss ongoing updates or the next improvements that make sense for your business.
