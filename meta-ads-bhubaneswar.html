COMMON QUESTIONS

Meta Ads questions

What should be ready before a Meta Ads campaign starts?

Agree on the campaign objective, audience, offer, creative, destination page and how enquiries or other outcomes will be measured. Access to the relevant advertising account is also needed for campaign setup.

Can Meta Ads guarantee sales or enquiries?

No. Advertising performance depends on factors such as the market, audience, budget, offer, creative and page experience. We can plan, monitor and adjust a campaign, but should not promise a specific result.

How do you decide if an ad is working?

Start with the campaign goal and review the related measures, such as relevant visits, completed enquiry forms or other agreed actions. Reach and clicks alone do not show whether the campaign is producing useful business outcomes.
