COMMON QUESTIONS

Social media marketing questions

Which social channels should my business use?

Choose channels based on where your intended customers spend time and what each channel helps you communicate. It is usually more useful to manage a small number of relevant profiles consistently than to spread effort across every platform.

What should a social media content plan include?

A practical plan can include recurring topics, formats, a manageable publishing schedule, clear calls to action and a process for responding to customer questions. It should reflect your actual services and offers.

How can I tell whether social media is helping?

Choose measures connected to your goal, such as relevant profile visits, website clicks, messages or enquiries. Review those signals over time and adjust the content rather than relying only on follower counts.
