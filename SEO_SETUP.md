# SEO setup and follow-up

## Website changes included

- The homepage and every service page use a self-referencing canonical URL.
- The sitemap lists the homepage and all seven service pages. Its `lastmod` values reflect this material content update; update them only when a page changes significantly.
- `robots.txt` allows crawling and points to the sitemap.
- The homepage describes KalingaX Digital as an `Organization`. A street address was not added because the website currently gives only Bhubaneswar, Odisha; do not publish an address unless it is accurate and intended to be public.
- Homepage and service pages have unique titles and descriptions, canonical URLs, social-share image metadata, and service-specific content.
- The hero photo and logo have optimized WebP versions; the hero uses responsive image sources and descriptive alt text.
- Service pages include original, service-specific answers to common customer questions. No testimonials, ratings, portfolio items, or client logos are fabricated.
- The portfolio and testimonial sections were removed until real work and customer feedback are available.

## Google Search Console

1. Add and verify the `kalingaxdigital.com` domain property in Google Search Console.
2. Submit `https://kalingaxdigital.com/sitemap.xml` in the Sitemaps report.
3. Use URL Inspection for the homepage and each service page. If a page is available and indexable, request indexing after deployment.
4. Check the Pages, HTTPS, and Core Web Vitals reports periodically and fix reported issues.
5. Verify the `www` and non-`www` hostnames as needed, and make sure the domain redirects consistently to the canonical hostname.

## Google Business Profile and local presence

1. Claim or create the profile for the real business and complete the business name, category, service area, phone, website, and hours accurately.
2. Keep the business name and contact information consistent with the website and other legitimate listings.
3. Ask actual customers for honest feedback. Do not create or purchase reviews.
4. Add real business and work photos where appropriate. Keep the service area accurate; do not publish a private address unless the business serves customers there and it is appropriate to display.

## Performance measurement

After deployment, check the homepage and service pages in PageSpeed Insights and review real-user data in Search Console when enough data is available. Prioritize any repeatedly reported mobile layout, image-loading, and Core Web Vitals issues.
