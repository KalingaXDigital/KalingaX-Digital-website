COMMON QUESTIONS

Instagram marketing questions

How often should a business post on Instagram?

There is no single schedule that fits every business. Choose a pace you can maintain while making each post useful, accurate and relevant to your audience. Consistency matters more than posting simply to fill a calendar.

What kinds of content can a business share?

Useful formats may include service explainers, answers to common customer questions, product or process details, and timely updates. The right mix depends on what you offer and what your audience wants to understand.

Can an Instagram plan guarantee follower or sales growth?

No posting plan can guarantee a specific number of followers, enquiries or sales. Results depend on the offer, audience, creative, consistency and other factors. We focus on clear content and reviewing relevant engagement and enquiry signals.
