COMMON QUESTIONS

Branding questions

What can a branding project include?

Depending on what your business needs, the work may cover positioning, key messages, visual direction and guidance for using the selected brand assets consistently. The final scope is agreed for each project.

Can you work with my existing logo and brand materials?

Yes. We can start by reviewing the assets you already use, identify what should stay consistent, and discuss whether any updates are useful for the goals of the project.

How do I keep my brand consistent online?

Use the same approved logo, colours, voice and core service descriptions across your website and social profiles. A simple set of brand notes can help everyone preparing future content follow the same direction.
